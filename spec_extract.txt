1. Executive Summary
Safari Land Driver-Guide Partner Platform converts the traditional paper sales coupon into a broader digital relationship and commerce platform. A verified driver-guide receives a permanent Safari Land identity and trackable referral assets. Guests can verify the guide, access multilingual safari stories and memories, receive a shopping pass and benefit, participate in a trip-specific farewell, submit verified feedback, optionally provide gratuity, preserve the relationship after travel, and share the guide profile or stories. Safari Land can attribute visits and purchases to the correct guide, calculate commission, manage payouts, and build a trusted directory of professional Tanzania guides.
MVP commercial loopVerified Driver → Referral Asset → Guest → Shopping Pass → Verified Visit → POS Sale → Commission → Wallet → Withdrawal.
MVP relationship loopTrip → Guide Stories → Personal Farewell → Guest Response → Verified Review → Optional Appreciation → Saved Memory → Shareable Guide Reputation.
Figure 1. Safari Land MVP ecosystem and primary application surfaces.
1.1 MVP Success Conditions
Every qualifying guest can be attributed to a verified driver and referral source.
Every qualifying POS sale can resolve the guest, driver, discount rule and commission rule.
The guide can create trip stories and farewell content without professional writing skills.
Guests can consume content and respond in a preferred supported language.
Reviews are tied to verified trips and cannot be selectively suppressed by the guide.
Gratuity is optional, separated from commission, and available only within configured rules.
The guide has a shareable professional profile based on verified and clearly marked self-declared data.
Administrative users have complete auditability over identity, referrals, sales, commissions, withdrawals, content and moderation.

2.2 Product Principles
One driver identity; many trackable referral assets.
One guest pass per referral context; no sensitive data embedded directly in QR codes.
The backend is the authoritative source for identity, attribution, money and status.
The guide gives value before asking the guest for feedback or optional appreciation.
AI assists expression and translation but does not invent memories, wildlife facts, credentials or guest statements.
Original content is preserved; cleaned and translated variants are versioned.
Guest-facing journeys must not require installation of a mobile application.
Private trip content and public professional content are distinct visibility classes.
Verified facts and self-declared facts must be visibly distinguishable.
Financial ledgers for sales commission and gratuity remain separate.

5. Driver Identity, Registration and Verification
FR-DRV-001 — Driver registration
The platform shall allow a prospective driver-guide to create an account using mobile number and OTP and submit a verification profile.
Acceptance criteria:
Required fields include full name, mobile number, WhatsApp number, profile photo and acceptance of terms.
Email, current company, guide licence/reference, languages and payment information may be collected as configured.
New registrations remain Pending until approved by authorized Safari Land staff.
FR-DRV-002 — Permanent Safari Land Driver ID
Upon approval the backend shall issue a non-reusable permanent identifier such as SL-DG-002851. The identifier remains stable when company, vehicle or contact details change.
FR-DRV-003 — Driver account states
Supported states shall include Pending, Active, Suspended, Rejected and Deactivated. Suspended/deactivated accounts shall not create new qualifying referrals or withdrawals.
FR-DRV-004 — Driver profile
The driver shall maintain permitted profile fields while protected fields such as member ID, verification state, commission configuration and system audit fields remain administrator-controlled.

6. QR, Referral Assets and Attribution
FR-QR-001 — Permanent profile QR
Each active driver shall receive a permanent QR that resolves to a secure Safari Land referral URL/token and opens the driver public/guest landing experience.
FR-QR-002 — Channel-specific referral assets
A driver may create or receive multiple child referral assets tagged by source such as Mobile, Vehicle, WhatsApp, Website, LinkedIn/Social, Business Card, Hotel/Flyer or Other. Each asset shall have a unique referral_asset_id and opaque token.
FR-QR-003 — Unlimited copies
Multiple printed or digital copies of the same referral asset shall resolve to the same asset ID. Separate assets are required only when separate performance tracking is required.
FR-QR-004 — Referral event
Each scan or click shall create a referral event/session containing driver ID, referral asset ID, source, timestamp, session identifier, device/browser metadata permitted by privacy policy and subsequent conversion state.
FR-QR-005 — Attribution rule engine
The backend shall apply configurable attribution validity and conflict rules. Overrides shall require authorized staff and an audit reason.
FR-QR-006 — QR security
QR codes shall not expose driver phone numbers, payment details, internal database keys or guest personal data. Tokens shall be opaque, revocable where applicable and validated server-side.

7. Tourist Experience and Shopping Pass
FR-GST-001 — Guide verification landing
A guest opening a driver referral shall see driver name, photo, verified Safari Land status, member ID or public member reference, applicable guest benefit and actions to obtain a Shopping Pass or explore Safari Land.
FR-GST-002 — Guest registration
The pass flow shall minimize friction. First name is mandatory; nationality/country is strongly structured; additional contact fields are configurable and consent-based.
FR-GST-003 — Individual Shopping Pass
The system shall issue a unique pass ID and QR linked to the guest, driver, referral asset/session, discount entitlement, creation time, expiry and status.
FR-GST-004 — Driver-created pass
The driver may create a pass for a guest who cannot or does not scan, using the same backend attribution rules.
FR-GST-005 — Pass retrieval
Guests shall be able to keep the pass via screenshot, saved web link or configured messaging/email delivery without mandatory app installation.
FR-GST-006 — Staff verification
Staff shall locate a pass by QR scan, pass code, guest name or driver and mark arrival subject to permissions.
FR-GST-007 — Safari Land discovery
The guest landing experience shall expose configurable Safari Land categories, selected products/offers, store information and guest benefit. Full e-commerce checkout is outside the MVP baseline.

8. Trip, Group and Guest Management
FR-TRP-001 — Create trip/group
A driver shall create a trip/group with name/label, start/end dates, guest count, primary nationality/language where known, and optional itinerary context.
FR-TRP-002 — Guest roster
The driver may add named guests and family/group relationships using minimal data. Guest profiles inside a trip are private by default.
FR-TRP-003 — Arrival notification
The driver shall notify Safari Land of an expected visit using guest count and ETA. Staff shall see incoming groups in an operations queue.
FR-TRP-004 — Trip state
The trip shall use controlled states: Upcoming, On Safari, Farewell Preparation, Farewell Ready, Appreciation Open, Completed and Archived.
FR-TRP-005 — Trip QR/session
A trip may have a trip-specific QR/link. Its destination shall be context-aware based on trip state and guest authorization.

9. Safari Stories and Memory Book
FR-MEM-001 — Capture Safari Story
The driver shall create a Safari Story using voice or text and attach up to six photos in the MVP. A story is linked to a trip and optionally to a public guide portfolio.
FR-MEM-002 — Voice capture
Voice recordings shall be uploaded securely with progress/retry behavior. The original audio shall be preserved as the source artifact.
FR-MEM-003 — AI professional cleanup
AI shall transcribe and improve grammar, structure and readability while preserving the driver’s meaning and not inventing facts. The output shall remain Draft until guide approval.
FR-MEM-004 — Story controls
The guide may edit, regenerate, save draft, approve, publish or unpublish own content subject to moderation and retention rules.
FR-MEM-005 — Memory Book
A trip shall present approved stories and selected memories chronologically. Private trip memories and public portfolio stories shall be distinct visibility modes.
FR-MEM-006 — Content limits
MVP limits shall support a configurable maximum of 3 minutes of source audio, 6 photos and approximately 300–500 words of approved story output per story. Daily publishing limits shall be configurable.
FR-MEM-007 — Sensitive location protection
Public content shall not expose precise sensitive wildlife locations where prohibited by Safari Land policy; generalized destination labels shall be supported.

10. Safari Farewell, Reviews and Appreciation
10.1 Farewell Preparation
FR-FAR-001 — Private farewell workspace
During an active trip, the guide shall privately capture memories and draft individual or group farewell messages. Guests shall not see draft content before activation.
FR-FAR-002 — Personalized messages
Farewell messages may be associated with a specific guest or the whole group and may include approved trip photos or memories.
FR-FAR-003 — AI wording assistance
AI may clean grammar, preserve humor and tone, shorten repetition and improve clarity. The guide remains the author and must approve the final message.
FR-FAR-004 — Farewell readiness
The app shall show completion status for planned individual/group messages and require final review before activation.
FR-FAR-005 — Activate Safari Farewell
Only the guide or authorized staff may activate Farewell for the trip. Activation changes the trip state and enables the guest-facing Farewell journey.
Figure 7. Safari Farewell from private guide preparation to saved guest relationship.
10.2 Guest Farewell Experience
FR-FAR-006 — Guest identification
A group Farewell may ask the guest to select or securely resolve their identity before revealing a personal message. One guest shall not gain unauthorized access to another guest’s private message.
FR-FAR-007 — Guest response
After viewing the guide message, the guest may write a reply, record a short voice message, add an allowed photo or choose Maybe Later.
FR-FAR-008 — Experience review
The guest may submit an overall guide rating and configured categories such as Knowledge, Professionalism, Hospitality, Communication and Safety & Care, plus whether they would travel with the guide again.
FR-FAR-009 — Verified review
A review linked to a valid trip shall be marked Verified Trip. The guide cannot delete or suppress unfavorable verified reviews. Moderation is controlled by Safari Land.
FR-FAR-010 — Testimonial consent
The system shall separately ask whether the guest permits a response/testimonial to appear publicly on the guide profile. Private messages remain private unless explicit permission is granted.
FR-FAR-011 — Save for later
The guest shall be able to preserve secure access to the Farewell and Memory Book for later viewing through a durable link/account mechanism consistent with privacy rules.
10.3 Safari Appreciation / Gratuity
FR-GRA-001 — Professional positioning
Gratuity shall appear only after the guest has received the Farewell and completed or skipped response/review steps. The interface shall clearly state that gratuity is optional.
FR-GRA-002 — Guest choices
The guest shall be offered: Leave Digital Gratuity, I’ll Give It Personally, or Finish/No Thank You. Selecting personal gratitude shall not request or disclose an intended cash amount.
FR-GRA-003 — Digital amount
Digital gratuity shall support configured fixed suggestions plus Other Amount, subject to currency, provider and policy limits. Restaurant-style percentage prompts are not required.
FR-GRA-004 — Gratuity window
Digital gratuity availability shall be controlled by a configurable post-trip window. Farewell content may remain available after the gratuity window closes.
FR-GRA-005 — Separate ledger
Gratuity transactions shall be recorded separately from Safari Land sales commission and shall have independent payment, fee, refund/reversal and reconciliation states.
FR-GRA-006 — Payment integration boundary
The MVP shall expose a payment-provider integration interface. Where live payment integration is not enabled, the gratuity action shall be feature-flagged or limited to configured supported methods; the system shall never simulate a successful payment.

11. Multilingual and AI Content Services
FR-AI-001 — Language selection
Guest-facing profile, story, memory, farewell, review prompts and Safari Land discovery content shall support a language selector and persist guest language preference where consent/session permits.
FR-AI-002 — Create once, deliver multilingual
The guide may create source content in a supported language. The system shall produce approved cleaned source text and translated presentation variants for configured guest languages.
FR-AI-003 — Original preservation
The platform shall store original audio/text, detected/source language, transcription, cleaned approved version, translation variants, model/provider metadata and version/status separately.
FR-AI-004 — Guest-to-guide translation
Guest replies/reviews may be written or recorded in the guest language. The guide shall be able to view the original plus a translated version in the guide’s preferred language.
FR-AI-005 — Human approval
AI-generated guide stories and farewell messages shall not publish without guide approval. AI-polished guest testimonials shall not publish without guest approval/consent.
FR-AI-006 — No factual invention
AI prompts and validation shall explicitly prohibit fabrication of wildlife events, locations, credentials, guest statements, dates, distances, ratings or achievements.
FR-AI-007 — Translation labeling
Where translated audio is introduced later, it shall be clearly labeled as AI-translated. MVP may provide original guide audio with translated text.

12. Guide Passport, Public Profile and Guide Directory
FR-PRO-001 — Guide Passport
Each active guide shall have a structured professional profile containing identity, verified status, experience, languages, specialties, destinations, approved public stories, verified reviews, recommendations and permitted credentials.
FR-PRO-002 — Verification labels
Profile data shall distinguish Verified, Safari Land Verified Activity and Self-Declared fields. Unverified claims shall not receive verified badges.
FR-PRO-003 — Public sharing
The guide shall share the profile through a stable public URL and supported share actions including WhatsApp, LinkedIn/social, email and copy link.
FR-PRO-004 — Story sharing
Approved public Safari Stories shall have individual shareable URLs that link back to the guide profile and Safari Land discovery experience.
FR-PRO-005 — Dreams & Goals
The guide may publish structured professional goals such as starting a tour business, seeking mentorship, partnerships, training, travel-industry opportunities or international exchange. Financial investment solicitation is not part of the MVP transaction flow.
FR-PRO-006 — Guide Directory
Safari Land shall provide a searchable/listable guide directory with configurable filters such as destination, language, specialty, experience, rating and verified status.
FR-PRO-007 — Professional discovery
A public profile may expose actions such as Recommend, Share Profile, Contact/Enquire, Work With Guide or Request Guide Again, subject to privacy and anti-spam controls.
FR-PRO-008 — Reputation metrics
Public reputation may include verified rating, verified guest count, recommendation rate, countries represented in aggregate, public stories and verified skills. Public gratuity totals and private financial information shall never be displayed.

13. Commerce, Commission, Wallet and Withdrawal
FR-COM-001 — POS sale attribution
A finalized qualifying sale shall reference the Shopping Pass/referral context and therefore resolve the credited driver. Manual reassignment requires privileged override and audit reason.
FR-COM-002 — Discount engine
Guest discount shall be resolved from configured driver/tier/campaign rules. The applied rule and amount shall be stored with the sale attribution record.
FR-COM-003 — Commission engine
Commission shall be calculated server-side using configurable eligible amount, rate, exclusions, tax treatment, returns and rounding rules. The client app shall never be authoritative for commission.
FR-COM-004 — Commission states
Commission states shall include Pending, Approved, Available, Withdrawal Requested, Paid, Reversed and Disputed as applicable.
FR-COM-005 — Driver wallet
The driver wallet shall show Available, Pending and Lifetime commission, transaction history and withdrawal status. Gratuity shall be displayed as a distinct balance/ledger category if enabled.
FR-COM-006 — Withdrawal request
The driver shall request withdrawal against available funds, choose an approved payout method/account and receive a unique request/reference ID.
FR-COM-007 — Manual finance MVP
The finance portal shall support manual payout processing: review → approve → pay externally → enter provider/reference → mark paid. Payment APIs may replace the external step without changing ledger semantics.
FR-COM-008 — Reversal
Voided/refunded qualifying sales shall generate commission reversal or adjustment according to configured business rules and preserve the audit trail.

16. Security, Privacy, Audit and Non-Functional Requirements
16.1 Security Requirements
All network traffic shall use TLS.
Sensitive tokens shall be opaque and validated server-side.
Admin and finance privileges shall use least-privilege RBAC; privileged accounts shall support MFA.
Payment card data shall not be stored by Safari Land unless the selected architecture is explicitly compliant; tokenized/provider-hosted payment methods are preferred.
Passwords, secrets and provider keys shall not be embedded in mobile/web clients.
Rate limiting and abuse controls shall protect OTP, QR resolution, review, contact and payment endpoints.
Financial and attribution overrides shall require reason capture and immutable audit events.
Uploaded media shall be scanned/validated for file type, size and malicious content.
16.2 Privacy and Consent Requirements
Guest data collection shall be minimized to the purpose of the journey.
Private trip messages are visible only to authorized trip participants and staff with support need.
Public testimonial publication requires explicit guest permission distinct from review submission.
Guest photos containing identifiable persons shall follow configured consent rules before public publication.
Public profiles shall not expose driver payout details, private phone numbers unless deliberately published, gratuity totals or private guest information.
Guests shall be able to withdraw public testimonial permission subject to retention/audit policy.
Aggregate country maps/metrics shall not reveal individual guest identities.
16.3 Non-Functional Requirements

Versioning

17.1 End-to-End MVP Acceptance Scenario
An administrator approves Jerome and the system issues a permanent Driver ID.
Jerome displays a Vehicle referral QR; Daniel scans it.
The backend records Jerome + Vehicle source and opens Jerome’s multilingual guest profile.
Daniel creates a Shopping Pass and saves it.
Jerome creates the Wilson Family trip and adds Daniel to the trip.
During the safari Jerome records a story; AI transcribes/cleans it; Jerome approves it; Daniel views it in his chosen language.
Jerome prepares Daniel’s personal Farewell message and activates Farewell at trip end.
Daniel opens the Farewell, replies, submits a verified review and grants or declines public testimonial permission.
Daniel optionally chooses digital gratuity, personal gratitude or Finish.
At Safari Land, staff scan Daniel’s Shopping Pass and mark Arrived.
A qualifying POS sale is finalized and linked to the pass.
The backend calculates commission and posts it Pending.
Authorized finance approves the commission; it becomes Available.
Jerome requests withdrawal; finance records the payout reference and marks Paid.
Admin analytics show the complete chain from Vehicle QR → Daniel → trip → visit → sale → commission, while private Farewell content remains protected.
